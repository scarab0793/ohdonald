Were U.S. jobs numbers 'rigged'?

WHEN & WHERE WAS IT SAID?

When: Aug 1, 2025

Where: Post on Truth Social

Original context / reporting ↗
THE CLAIM (BRACE YOURSELF)
The Bureau of Labor Statistics employment figures were phony or rigged

WHAT HE SAID (WORD FOR WORD, SADLY)

After a weak jobs report he accused the Bureau of Labor Statistics of producing manipulated numbers and fired its commissioner.

WHEN AND WHERE (THE RECEIPTS HAVE A CALENDAR)

Aug. 1, 2025, the day of the July jobs report, announced on his social media account. The commissioner, Erika McEntarfer, had been nominated by Biden and took office in January 2024; the deputy, William Wiatrowski, became acting director.

WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS (BRING SNACKS)

  • The report showed 73,000 jobs added in July and unemployment up to 4.2% from 4.1%.
  • It also revised May from 125,000 to 19,000 jobs and June from 147,000 to 14,000. Revisions of this kind are routine as more survey responses come in.
  • Economists and investors have long treated U.S. economic statistics as free of partisan interference; FactCheck.org found no evidence of political manipulation. U.S. stock indexes fell about 1.5% that day.

WHY THIS MATTERS

When data disappoint, you can improve the economy or fire the thermometer. Only one is a documented option here.

THE FACTS (SORRY)

No evidence of political manipulation was found; routine revisions are normal.
