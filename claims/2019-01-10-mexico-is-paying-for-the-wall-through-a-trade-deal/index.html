Mexico is 'paying' for the wall — through a trade deal?

WHEN & WHERE WAS IT SAID?

When: Jan 10, 2019

Where: Remarks in Texas during a border visit

Original context / reporting ↗
THE CLAIM (BRACE YOURSELF)
They're paying for the wall in a great trade deal
THE FACTS (SORRY)

The Associated Press found that Mexico has refused to pay anything toward the wall and that the updated trade deal contains no payment or refund for construction; the claimed economic benefits cannot be counted on or quantified. The original promise was that Mexico would pay directly.

Finding: MisleadingTechnically words. Technically.
