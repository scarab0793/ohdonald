THE CLAIM (BRACE YOURSELF)
We had the greatest economy in the history of our country

WHAT HE SAID (WORD FOR WORD, SADLY)

He said the country had "the greatest economy in the history of our country."

WHEN AND WHERE (THE RECEIPTS HAVE A CALENDAR)

FactCheck.org examined the claim in its October 2026 roundup of his midterm rally speeches. The source does not give the exact venue or time, and we do not guess.

WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS (BRING SNACKS)

  • FactCheck.org found that real GDP growth during Trump's first term was exceeded in many earlier years.
  • 'Greatest' is a superlative, so the check is the record: it is a comparison, and the comparison fails on the growth numbers FactCheck.org cites.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Superlatives are opinions until someone puts them next to a historical series. Then they are claims, and this one lost the comparison.

THE FACTS (SORRY)

Real GDP growth during Trump's first term was exceeded in many earlier years.
