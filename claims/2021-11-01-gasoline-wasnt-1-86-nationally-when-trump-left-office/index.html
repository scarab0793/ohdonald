Economy · Dec 8, 2021 · False

Gasoline wasn't $1.86 nationally when Trump left office

WHEN & WHERE WAS IT SAID?

When: Dec 8, 2021

Where: Interview with Hugh Hewitt

Original context / reporting ↗
THE CLAIM (BRACE YOURSELF)
Gas prices were $1.86 when I left the White House

WHAT HE SAID (WORD FOR WORD, SADLY)

He said that gasoline was $1.86 a gallon when he left the White House: "It was $1.86 when I left."

WHEN AND WHERE (THE RECEIPTS HAVE A CALENDAR)

Interview with Hugh Hewitt, Dec. 8, 2021, during a discussion of rising gas prices under Biden.

WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS (BRING SNACKS)

  • The Energy Information Administration put the national average at $2.38 for the week of Jan. 18, 2021, about 28% higher than his figure.
  • The national average was that low under Trump only in March 2020, when pandemic lockdowns collapsed demand.
  • California averaged $3.21 that week; Texas just under $2.10; Houston $2.06. GasBuddy's Patrick De Haan said no U.S. city or state averaged $1.86 on Jan. 20, 2021. PolitiFact rated the claim False.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A cheap price at one station in one week is an anecdote, not a national average.

THE FACTS (SORRY)

PolitiFact reported a national average of roughly $2.38 per gallon.

Finding: FalseBold. Wrong, but bold.
