Elections · Nov 4, 2020 · False

Declaring victory before votes were counted

WHEN & WHERE WAS IT SAID?

When: Nov 4, 2020

Where: East Room of the White House, about 2:21 a.m. ET

Original context / reporting ↗
THE CLAIM (BRACE YOURSELF)
Frankly, we did win this election

WHAT HE SAID (WORD FOR WORD, SADLY)

He said, "We already have won" the election, and added, "Frankly, we did win this election."

WHEN AND WHERE (THE RECEIPTS HAVE A CALENDAR)

East Room of the White House, Nov. 4, 2020. His remarks began at 2:21 a.m. Eastern time.

WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS (BRING SNACKS)

  • Many votes were still uncounted, including mail and absentee ballots from urban areas likely to favor Biden; Biden was gaining in Georgia.
  • No mainstream media decision desk had called the race for Trump, nor had Fox News, Newsmax or OANN. PolitiFact rated it Pants on Fire.
  • Federal law gives states more than a month to finalize results; electors vote on Dec. 14 and Congress counts them Jan. 6. Later certified results showed Biden won.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Declaring victory is easy. Counting is the hard part.

THE FACTS (SORRY)

Trump declared victory before all ballots had been counted; later certified results showed Biden won.

Finding: FalseBold. Wrong, but bold.
