WHAT HE SAID (WORD FOR WORD, SADLY)

He said, "this Russia thing with Trump and Russia is a made up story." PolitiFact named 'Russian election interference is a made-up story' its 2017 Lie of the Year.

WHEN AND WHERE (THE RECEIPTS HAVE A CALENDAR)

NBC News interview with Lester Holt at the White House, May 11, 2017. PolitiFact's award is dated Dec. 12, 2017.

WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS (BRING SNACKS)

  • U.S. intelligence agencies concluded in January 2017 that Russia interfered in the 2016 election.
  • PolitiFact's Lie of the Year concerned the repeated dismissal of the interference as 'made up', not individual contacts.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Whether anyone colluded was a separate question. That interference happened was not.
