WHEN AND WHERE (THE RECEIPTS HAVE A CALENDAR)

Interview with Sean Hannity on Fox News, July 9, 2020.

WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS (BRING SNACKS)

  • He was describing a cognitive screening test, not an intelligence test.
  • A high score on a cognitive screen cannot by itself establish exceptional intelligence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Passing a screening and a measure of intelligence are not the same thing.

THE FACTS (SORRY)

Trump said he aced a cognitive assessment. Such screening assesses cognitive functioning rather than IQ; a high score alone cannot establish exceptional intelligence.
