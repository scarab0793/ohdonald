# OhDonald.com — U.S. launch kit

## Positioning

**He said it. Then he said the opposite. We kept the receipts.**

OhDonald is an independent, source-led archive of Donald Trump's public claims and changes in position. It distinguishes direct contradictions from changes of opinion and comparisons between promises and later policy actions. Satire belongs in the presentation; factual conclusions require evidence.

**Primary destination:** https://ohdonald.com/#pairs

## Launch checklist

1. Verify each featured quote against an original transcript, video, or archived post; verify both dates and source links. Remove or relabel entries that cannot be substantiated.
2. Test the live English homepage, contradiction anchors, mobile layout, and share buttons after GitHub Pages deployment.
3. Check the Open Graph preview on Bluesky, Reddit and other platforms. The homepage already points to /og.png; test the actual image before posting.
4. Register the domain with Google Search Console, verify domain ownership, submit https://ohdonald.com/sitemap.xml, and check indexing. Domain verification requires the site owner's DNS access.
5. Select a privacy-respecting analytics provider and add its authorized script only after an account and site ID exist. Do not fabricate traffic or geographic audience numbers.
6. Use a tagged URL for each permitted post so referral performance can be compared.

## Measuring results

Record, by week: unique visits, U.S. traffic share (only if the analytics provider reports it), source/referrer, visits to the contradiction tracker, outbound source clicks, and returning visitors. Keep a note of which posts were actually published and where.

**Do not claim the campaign is live until an external post has actually been published.**

## Editorial safeguards

- Never invent quotations, exact dates, locations, or original source URLs.
- If a source is secondary, identify it as such; do not call it an original transcript.
- Distinguish a change of mind from a provable contradiction.
- Keep corrections visible and avoid inflammatory or personally targeted language.
- Do not buy fake traffic, use bots to amplify engagement, or spam communities.
