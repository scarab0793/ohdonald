OUR STANDARDS

Methodology

Evidence before punchlines.

We distinguish checkable factual claims from opinion, prediction and satire. Every published verdict must include dated evidence and source links. Primary sources are preferred; reputable independent fact-checking can provide context.

Verdicts

False: contradicted by reliable evidence. Misleading: technically selective or lacking material context. Needs context: interpretation requires qualification. Supported: substantiated by the available evidence.

Corrections

New evidence may change a verdict. Updates and corrections should be logged with dates and explanations. Unreviewed items are not published as fact checks.

Contradiction tracker

Every comparison requires two attributable statements, dates and direct source links. We preserve context and distinguish genuine factual conflict from changes in circumstances, policies or emphasis. We do not speculate about intent, memory or health.

Satire

Editorial jokes are clearly separated from factual findings. A joke is never a source. We do not infer a person's intent or medical condition from a claim.
